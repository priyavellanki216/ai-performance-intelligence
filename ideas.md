# Chosen direction: Evidence Ledger

## Design Movement

**Editorial systems design** meets archival research interfaces. The visual language borrows from a well-designed analyst brief: strong typographic hierarchy, data as evidence, and visual restraint that makes complicated AI workflow feel intelligible.

## Core Principles

1. **Evidence before assertion.** Metrics and generated-review claims always carry a source cue, method label, or verification state.
2. **Asymmetric clarity.** Use a generous editorial column for narrative paired with compact evidence panels, not a centered marketing grid.
3. **Human oversight is visible.** Approval gates and citations are treated as the visual center of the product story, not as an afterthought.
4. **Technical precision without coldness.** Fine rules, mono metadata, and subtle motion live alongside warm paper tones and human-centered language.

## Color Philosophy

The core palette is **warm ledger paper** and near-black ink, designed to evoke a trustworthy review record rather than a futuristic AI sales page. A saturated **verdict blue** acts as the ownable accent: it signals verified evidence, clear system state, and forward movement without slipping into neon. Rust is reserved for validation flags; sage signals approved or cached outcomes.

## Layout Paradigm

The page unfolds as a vertical case file with an off-axis left rail for section numbering and metadata. Large narrative blocks alternate with stacked evidence strips and technical cards. Sections are intentionally misaligned at desktop to create a reading rhythm instead of a conventional centered landing-page grid.

## Signature Elements

1. **Evidence stamps:** Small outlined status chips such as “Source-linked,” “Validated,” and “Benchmark scenario.”
2. **Ledger rules:** Hairline borders, numbered section markers, and marginal notes give each block an audit-ready character.
3. **Data contours:** A restrained dotted contour field and soft concentric arcs recur in the hero and outcome panels, representing synthesis from scattered feedback.

## Interaction Philosophy

Interactions behave like handling a carefully indexed research file. Anchors glide to sections; cards lift by a few pixels and intensify their evidence rule on hover; expandable details reveal method notes without hiding primary conclusions. No interaction should imply that AI operates without review.

## Animation

Use brief, low-motion transitions with a crisp ease-out. On entry, rules extend horizontally and facts fade upward by 8px in a staggered sequence. The hero’s contour field shifts very slowly only when reduced motion is not requested. Hover transitions stay below 180ms; all motion is disabled or reduced under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Typography System

**DM Serif Display** is used sparingly for high-stakes narrative statements and section titles. **Manrope** provides legible, modern body copy and navigation. **IBM Plex Mono** labels metrics, status chips, technologies, and method notes. Headlines are strong but never oversized enough to obscure the evidence architecture.

## Brand Essence

**A verifiable AI performance-review workspace for people teams that need a draft they can defend.**

Personality: **considered, accountable, lucid**.

## Brand Voice

Headlines are specific, analytical, and quietly confident. CTAs invite inspection rather than pushing conversion. Microcopy makes evaluation criteria and review ownership explicit.

Example lines:

> “Every sentence begins with a source, not a hunch.”

> “Draft faster. Approve with evidence.”

## Wordmark & Logo

The **Verity** mark is a cobalt verification check constructed from three staggered ledger lines inside an open archival bracket. It has no text in the symbol itself; the logotype pairs the mark with a custom, letter-spaced “VERITY // PERFORMANCE INTELLIGENCE” wordmark.

## Signature Brand Color

**Verdict Blue — #2456E8.** A high-contrast cobalt used only for key anchors, verified states, and the brand mark.

# Metric treatment

No user-provided production telemetry was supplied. The website therefore presents the numerical values as a **clearly labeled benchmark scenario**, derived from a reproducible pre-production test plan rather than claimed customer or employment outcomes. Before use in a resume, the owner should replace these values with their measured workload, latency percentile, evaluation sample, cache hit-rate, test coverage, and deployment evidence.
